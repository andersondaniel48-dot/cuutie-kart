How to play

TouchYour kart drives itself forward. Drag on the left side to steer. Hold DRIFT while turning to slide, then let go for a mini-turbo. Tap ITEM to use what you picked up. KeysArrows or WASD to drive and steer · Space to hop and drift · Shift or E for items · P to pause DriftSparks turn blue, then pink, then violet. Release on violet for the longest boost. RampsPress DRIFT in mid-air to do a trick. You get a small boost when you land. HeartsEach heart you collect adds a little top speed, up to 10. Getting hit drops three.
